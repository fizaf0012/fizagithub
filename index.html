FINAL YEAR PROJECT

PolicyPulse

An AI-powered public opinion analysis platform that collects multilingual policy-related data and analyzes public sentiment using Natural Language Processing.

Project Overview

PolicyPulse is my Final Year Project (FYP), developed to analyze public opinion about government policies and public issues, with a focus on health and education. It uses NLP and AI to classify public feedback into positive, negative, and neutral sentiments.

Key Features

  • Multilingual analysis of Urdu, Roman Urdu, and English.
  • Positive, negative, and neutral sentiment classification.
  • Interactive sentiment dashboards and trend analysis.
  • Policy comparison and regional analysis.
  • AI-powered chatbot for policy-related queries.
  • Role-based access for different user types.

My Contribution
